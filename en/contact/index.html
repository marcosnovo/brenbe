Contact

Tell me which room you want to refresh and we will figure out how to help. The first conversation is about understanding your starting point and deciding whether an online design consultation is what you need.

What to send to get started

You don't need to have it all figured out. This is enough to begin:

  • Photos of the room from several angles and, if possible, at different times of day.
  • Approximate measurements: length, width and ceiling height, plus where the doors and windows are. Feet and inches are fine.
  • What you want to change and what you want to keep: furniture, colors, pieces that matter to you.
  • Inspiration you like: photos, colors, rooms.
  • Your approximate budget for furniture and decor, even as a range.
  • Whether you rent or own, so I know which changes are on the table.

What happens next

  1. You email me or message me on WhatsApp with your question and photos of the space.
  2. I tell you how I work and which service fits what you need.
  3. If it feels right, we agree on the scope and a call time that works in your time zone, and we get started.

Online and in person

The online consultation takes place by video call from Argentina and works for any city or country. Argentina is on UTC-3 year-round, so I am 1 to 2 hours ahead of Eastern Time and 4 to 5 hours ahead of Pacific Time depending on daylight saving time.{% if c_ciudad != '' %} I also see clients in person in {{ c_ciudad | escape }}{% if c_prov != '' %}, {{ c_prov | escape }}{% endif %}, Argentina.{% endif %} Every service is no-renovation: no construction and no contractor supervision.
