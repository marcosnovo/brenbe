Online interior design consultation

A virtual interior design consultation to sort out your ideas and decide how to refresh your home, without a renovation. I'm Brenda: I work from Argentina by video call with clients in the US and around the world, in English or Spanish, and we build the plan around the room you want to improve. What an e-design consultation includes

If your living room, bedroom, dining area or home office needs a change, we can look at how you use it and which adjustments will have the most impact. It is a good fit for renters, first-time homeowners, anyone furnishing a small apartment, and anyone who wants a professional point of view before spending money on paint or furniture.

  • Layout and furniture. We review traffic flow, measurements and which pieces you already own can keep working.
  • Color, paint and wallpaper. We define a palette that takes your light and surfaces into account.
  • Lighting, textiles and styling. We combine pieces that suit how you use the space and the style you want.

How it works, step by step

  1. You share your starting point. We talk through the room, photos, measurements and your priorities, including your budget range.
  2. We set a visual direction. We build a palette and choose references so every decision follows a shared idea.
  3. You leave with concrete recommendations. A curated selection of pieces and styling adjustments you can shop for and set up at your own pace.

Time zones and scheduling

Argentina is on UTC-3 all year with no daylight saving time. That puts me 1 to 2 hours ahead of the US East Coast and 4 to 5 hours ahead of the West Coast, depending on the time of year. We agree on a call time that is comfortable in your local time, and most of the work (photos, measurements, notes) can be shared in writing whenever suits you.

What it does not include

This is a no-renovation service. It does not cover construction, contractor supervision or structural advice. It also does not replace a licensed professional where your local rules require one. If your project needs that, I will tell you up front.
