Which service is right for me?

If you are unsure, start with the online consultation. We look at the whole room and set priorities together. The focused services work best when you already know what you want to solve, such as a paint color or where the furniture should go. If your space is tiny, the small apartment and studio service puts it all together for a few square feet.

Every service is no-renovation: no construction, no contractor management and no structural work. We work with decor, color, furniture, lighting and textiles, which is what lets you update a room without touching a wall, and it makes the whole approach renter-friendly.
