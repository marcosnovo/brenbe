Color is the decorating decision you notice most and fear most: paint shows up instantly and is a hassle to undo. That is why so many people pick "builder white just in case" and end up with a room that doesn't feel like theirs. A color consultation is a way to move forward with a method, without getting stuck between forty swatches that all look the same.

## Who it's for

This consultation helps if you:

- Are about to paint a room (or your whole home) and don't know which shade to pick.
- Have furniture, flooring or windows you won't change and want color that works with them.
- Just moved and want to start with a cohesive palette.
- Rent and need to solve color with textiles, rugs and objects.
- Already painted and the result didn't feel right.

## What we work on

**Your light.** Which way the windows face, what time of day you spend the most time in each room and the type of artificial lighting all change how a color looks. We start there.

**Your existing materials.** Flooring, wood tones, finishes and window frames have undertones that pair well with some palettes and clash with others.

**The whole palette.** It's not just the wall color. We define a base color, one or two secondary colors and accents, and how they are spread across walls, textiles, furniture and objects so everything reads as one.

**Flow between rooms.** When we work on more than one room, we make sure the colors talk to each other and the move from one room to the next feels natural.

## What you get

- A clear color direction for the room or rooms we work on.
- A palette with base, secondary and accent tones, and how to use them.
- Visual references so you can see where the proposal is heading.
- Recommendations for testing color on your wall before painting everything.
- Suggestions for pairing the palette with textiles, rugs and objects.

## How it works

1. **You show me your space.** You share photos of the room at different times of day, rough measurements and what you want to keep.
2. **We set the direction.** We talk about how you want the room to feel and I put together a palette proposal.
3. **You test and decide.** I guide you through sampling and adjusting before you buy the paint.

## What it doesn't include

This is a no-renovation service. It does not include the painting itself or supervising any work. I tell you what to choose and how to test it, and whoever paints is up to you.
