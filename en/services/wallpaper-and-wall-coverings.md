A well-chosen wallpaper gives a room character in a way paint alone can't: texture, pattern, depth. It is also one of the hardest decisions to visualize on a wall and one of the costliest to fix. This consultation walks with you from the first idea to a concrete choice.

## Who it's for

- You want to give a wall personality (headboard wall, living room, entryway, home office) and don't know which pattern to pick.
- You are torn between wallpaper, paint, paneling and other wall coverings.
- You have a small room and worry a pattern will overwhelm it.
- You already chose a wallpaper and want to confirm it works with your furniture, light and palette.

## What we work on

**Where to use it.** Not every wall needs the same treatment. A feature wall, the wall behind a bed or a reading nook are often good spots; in other cases a deep paint color on a full wall works better than a pattern.

**The scale of the design.** Pattern size, the distance between repeats and the contrast with the background all change the feel. A large print can overpower a small room, and a very fine one can disappear in a big one.

**How it relates to color and furniture.** A wallpaper doesn't live alone: it sits next to flooring, textiles, wood tones and light. We think about the whole so the pieces support each other instead of competing.

**Alternatives.** Sometimes the best answer isn't wallpaper. It could be a limewash-style paint finish, wood paneling, a tall wainscot or a fabric wall covering. We compare options based on how the room is used and what you want to achieve.

**The condition of the wall.** An uneven wall, damp patches or peeling paint will affect the result. I tell you what to check before covering it.

## What you get

- A clear recommendation of where to use wallpaper or wall coverings and where not to.
- Criteria for choosing scale, color and type of pattern for your room.
- A proposal for how to combine it with your palette and furniture.
- Notes on what to review with whoever installs it.

## How it works

1. **You share your space.** Photos of the wall and the whole room, measurements and any options you have already seen.
2. **We analyze and propose.** We evaluate how each option behaves with your light and furniture.
3. **You decide with confidence.** You leave with a proposal you can act on at your own pace, with the wallpaper or covering that suits you best.

It does not include installation or wall preparation. This is a no-renovation advisory service: we choose together, and who does the work is up to you.
