Often a room "doesn't work" not because of a lack of furniture or style, but because the pieces are in the wrong place, the wrong size, or don't match how you live. This consultation focuses on exactly that: organizing the space, improving traffic flow and choosing the pieces you truly need.

## Who it's for

- You moved and don't know how to arrange what you own in the new space.
- You are about to buy a major piece (sofa, table, bed, bookshelf) and want to choose well.
- Your living or dining room feels awkward, cramped or messy and you can't tell why.
- You have a room that serves several functions and needs organizing.
- You want to refresh a space without buying everything new.

## What we work on

**Measurements and traffic flow.** We review the room plan, the paths between doors and zones, and how the furniture relates. A good layout lets you move comfortably, makes the most of natural light and gives every activity a logical place.

**What to keep and what to add.** We take inventory of your furniture: what works, what can move and what should be replaced or complemented. That way you don't spend where you don't need to.

**How to choose each piece.** For what you do need to buy, we define dimensions, proportions, materials and style, such as the sofa's size relative to the wall, the length of a dining table or the height of a bookshelf.

**Zones within one space.** In open-plan living/dining rooms, studios or a desk in the living room, we work on how to separate and connect zones with rugs, low furniture, lighting and the orientation of the pieces.

**Style.** The layout and pieces support the way you want the room to feel. The goal isn't to fill a space but to give every piece a reason to be there.

## What you get

- A layout proposal for the room, with recommended placements.
- A list of what to keep, move and add, with size and style criteria.
- Recommendations for the missing pieces, adapted to your budget.
- Guidelines for moving through and living in the space comfortably.

## How it works

1. **You tell me how you live in the space.** You share photos, measurements and which activities you want to solve.
2. **I analyze and propose.** I review the current layout and draft alternatives using your furniture and any needed pieces.
3. **You put it into practice.** With the recommendations, you can move the furniture and make purchases at your own pace.

This service can be part of the [online interior design consultation]({% include l.html ref='asesoria-online' %}) or focus on a single room. If the space you are solving is very tight, also see [small apartment and studio decorating]({% include l.html ref='servicio-deptos' %}).

## What it doesn't include

It does not include building custom furniture or supervising carpenters, and no walls or utilities are modified. This is a no-renovation consultation.
