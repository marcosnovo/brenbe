A studio or small apartment brings together in a few square feet what other homes spread across several rooms. When everything happens in one space, whatever isn't planned shows right away: no storage, the bed takes over the living area, nowhere to work. This consultation is designed to sort those decisions out so living in a small space feels comfortable.

## Who it's for

- You have a studio, a one-bedroom or a small apartment and feel like "it all doesn't fit".
- You are moving to a smaller place than your last one.
- You rent and want to improve the space without renovating or risking your security deposit.
- You work or study from home and need a corner that works.
- You want your apartment to feel more open, tidy and yours.

## What we work on

**Zone layout.** We define where each function goes (sleeping, lounging, eating, working) and how to separate them without walls, using rugs, low furniture, light curtains and lighting.

**Storage.** We review what you store and where: closet, furniture with a second function, high shelves, space under the bed. A small apartment looks tidy when everything has a home.

**The right furniture.** We choose pieces with the right size and proportions, visually light, that solve more than one need and don't block traffic flow.

**Color and light to gain a sense of space.** A short, cohesive palette, good curtains, well-placed mirrors and layered lighting make the space feel larger and warmer.

**Renter-friendly solutions.** Reversible options that add character without touching walls or wiring.

## What you get

- A layout plan for the zones in your apartment.
- Storage and furniture recommendations with dimensions (in feet and inches, with metric if you prefer).
- A palette and a proposal for lighting and textiles.
- A priority list so you can move forward in stages, according to your budget.

## How it works

1. **You show me your apartment.** Photos, measurements, a floor plan if you have one and a list of what you want to solve.
2. **We design the proposal.** I work out the layout, storage, color and light around your furniture and your budget.
3. **You get started.** With clear recommendations you can move ahead in stages, starting with what has the most impact.

## What it doesn't include

It does not include construction, custom carpentry or changes to utilities. We work with decor and furniture, which is what lets you refresh a space without breaking anything.
