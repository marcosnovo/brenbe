A room can have a good layout and good color and still feel cold or unfinished. Usually what is missing is what brings it to life: well-planned lighting, textiles and objects that tell a story. This consultation takes care of that final layer, the one that finishes a space and makes it feel cozy, and it needs no renovation.

## Who it's for

- Your room is organized but "something is missing".
- The light in your home feels cold, flat or not enough.
- You don't know which curtains, rug or throw pillows to choose.
- You want to refresh a space on a small budget without touching the walls.
- You struggle to decide which objects and wall art to display and how to arrange them.

## What we work on

**Layered lighting.** One ceiling light is not enough. We build a combination of general, task (reading, work) and ambient light (table lamps, floor lamps, plug-in sconces), with the right color temperature and brightness for each use. All with solutions that don't require changing your wiring.

**Curtains and natural light control.** We choose the curtain type, length, fabric and system (rod, track, roller shade) based on light, privacy and the room's style.

**Rugs and textiles.** A rug defines a zone and softens the space; throw pillows, blankets and bedding add color and texture. We watch sizes, materials and combinations so they add to the room rather than overload it.

**Objects, wall art and plants.** We select and group the pieces that give a room personality: how to hang wall art, which heights to combine on a shelf, how many objects are enough. Often less is more.

**Consistency with everything else.** It is all considered alongside your palette, furniture and the style you want, so the room feels unified.

## What you get

- A lighting proposal with the types of fixtures and where to place them.
- Curtain, rug and textile recommendations, with sizes and materials.
- A selection of objects and criteria for arranging them.
- A plan to add pieces gradually, in order of priority.

## How it works

1. **You share the room.** Photos by day and by night, measurements and the pieces you already own.
2. **We build the proposal.** I work out the lighting, textiles and objects in relation to the space and your palette.
3. **You put it in place.** With the recommendations, you can add pieces at your own pace, starting with the ones that change the room most.

You can add this service to a full [online interior design consultation]({% include l.html ref='asesoria-online' %}). If you are also reviewing your palette, the [color consultation]({% include l.html ref='servicio-color' %}) complements it well.

## What it doesn't include

It does not include changes to your electrical wiring or installing fixtures that require an electrician. This is a no-renovation service: I guide you on what to choose and how to combine it.
