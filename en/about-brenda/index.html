I help people refresh their homes with clear decisions on color, furniture, lighting and textiles, without renovating. I work online with clients in the US and around the world, in English or Spanish.

{% if s_ret != '' %}{% endif %}

How I work

A room should respond to how you live, not to a magazine photo. So I start by listening: how you use each space, what you love, what you want to keep and what drives you crazy. Then I build a visual direction with a color palette, references and a curated selection of pieces, so you can bring it to life at your own pace and on your own budget.

My work is consultation-based, which means no renovation: the change comes from color, furniture, lighting, textiles and objects. It is a faster and more accessible way to update a space, and the most comfortable one if you rent.

Why work with me online

Working over video call means you can get a personal design plan wherever you live, with no travel and no minimum project size. We share photos, measurements and notes, talk through the room together, and you leave with a plan you understand and can carry out yourself. I speak English fluently, and I also work in Spanish, which is handy if you live in a bilingual household or prefer to discuss your home in Spanish.

What I do and what I don't

  • I do: 100% online interior design and decorating consultations; paint color and wallpaper advice; furniture layout and selection; lighting, textiles and styling.
  • I don't do: construction management, renovations or structural calculations. If your project needs those, I will tell you right away.
