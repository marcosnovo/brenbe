Privacy and statistics

This site measures how many people visit it so we know which pages are useful. Here is, plainly, what is measured and what is not.

What is measured

Each time you open a page, an anonymous record is saved with: the page you viewed and its title, the date and time, the site you came from (for example Google or Pinterest) and, if the link carried one, its campaign (UTM); the approximate country, region and city inferred from your connection; the type of device (phone, tablet or computer); whether it looks like a robot; how long you stayed on the page and how far you scrolled; and whether you tapped the WhatsApp button or the contact email.

What is not done

  • No cookies are used.
  • Your IP address is not stored.
  • You are not identified and no profile of you is built: to link the pages of one visit, a random number is used that lives only in that browser tab and is erased when you close it.
  • This data is not shared with advertising networks.

The city is approximate

The location is inferred from your internet connection and may not match where you are: sometimes it is your provider's city.

Other tools

Cloudflare Web Analytics (also cookie-free) may be used as well, along with Google Search Console, which shows Brenda which searches led people to the site. That data is aggregated and comes from Google: it is not about your individual visit. WhatsApp and email work under their own policies.

Your choice

If your browser has "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control turned on, the site does not measure your visit.

How long it is kept

Records are deleted automatically after 400 days.
